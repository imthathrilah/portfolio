EcoTrash

The Intelligent Circular Economy Ecosystem
IoT Integration NodeMCU ESP8266 Firebase Android App WaaS

Project Overview

Originally conceived as a high-distinction Final Year Project at the University of Colombo, EcoTrash has scaled into a proprietary "Waste-as-a-Service" (WaaS) ecosystem under the Tetranix startup. It bridges the gap between inefficient urban waste collection and the global circular economy by transforming urban refuse into a traceable, profitable commodity.

The Problem

Urban areas face a severe waste crisis characterized by "Organic Decay" and the "Single-Bin Culture." Mixing organic waste with recyclables drops material market value by up to 80%. Furthermore, municipal collection teams follow fixed, "blind" routes regardless of actual bin fill levels, leading to high fuel costs, unnecessary carbon emissions, and public health risks from overflowing dumpsites.

Technical Architecture

  • Hardware: NodeMCU ESP8266 microcontrollers interfaced with HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors for volume tracking and HX711 load cells for precise weight measurement. Automated solenoid doors utilize limit switches for reliable positioning.
  • Backend Protocol: Firebase Realtime Database acts as the central hub, utilizing WebSockets for sub-500ms data synchronization and HTTP long-polling as a fallback for unstable network connectivity.
  • Software Interface: A fully customized Android application and Web Dashboard developed from scratch. Features distinct interfaces for end-users (B2C) to track waste and earnings, and collection teams (B2B) to view route optimization data.
  • At-Source Processing: Features integrated motorized crushing mechanisms for volume reduction and a unique thermal drying process to eliminate moisture and odors from organic waste.

Outcomes & Commercial Vision

Extensive hardware validation achieved a 95% accuracy rate in fill-level detection and a 98% accuracy rate in mass measurement. The project has evolved into a "Waste-to-Wealth" financial engine, incentivizing proper segregation by converting verified waste weight into digital cash payouts. Tetranix is currently targeting Institutional Pilots (Q3 2026) to deploy industrial-grade "Eco-Stations" before a widespread urban rollout.
