EcoWay

Hybrid Energy Harvesting & Air Purification
SOLIDWORKS CFD Analysis Sustainability Energy Harvesting

Project Overview

Developed under the Tetranix startup initiative, EcoWay is an innovative, dual-purpose infrastructure solution. It is engineered to harvest renewable energy generated by the aerodynamic turbulence of passing highway vehicles while simultaneously functioning as an active air purification system for high-emission transport corridors.

The Challenge

Highways and expressways generate immense amounts of unutilized kinetic energy in the form of wind wakes from fast-moving vehicles. Simultaneously, these same corridors are major sources of particulate matter and carbon emissions, contributing heavily to surrounding environmental degradation.

Engineering Methodology

  • CAD & Aerodynamic Modeling: Designed and simulated custom vertical-axis wind turbine (VAWT) blades using SOLIDWORKS, optimized to capture multi-directional wind wakes generated by vehicle traffic.
  • Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD): Extensive CFD analysis was utilized to determine the optimal placement, spacing, and blade geometry required to maximize energy extraction without creating hazardous drag for drivers.
  • Hybrid Power Management: The captured kinetic energy is converted and stored in a decentralized battery bank. This energy is then routed to power both the integrated highway lighting and the active air purification filters.
  • Air Purification Integration: The system forces the polluted highway air through an internal filtration matrix using the harvested mechanical power, scrubbing particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10) before releasing clean air back into the environment.

Impact & Sustainability

EcoWay acts as an off-grid, self-sustaining loop. By capitalizing on the otherwise wasted energy of highway traffic, the system eliminates the grid-reliance of highway infrastructure while actively lowering the carbon footprint of the transportation sector. It represents a step forward in passive, intelligent civil engineering.
